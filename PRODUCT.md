# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Framework, user's choice between Astro and Next. Delegated pick: Astro (static output, one page, no runtime needed; Next stays available if the site grows).

## Users

Players of a Minecraft roleplay server, residents of the in-fiction nation of Perland. They arrive from a Discord link or a shout in chat, on desktop or phone, and need to know in a minute what this political party stands for and whom to talk to in-game. Language: Spanish.

## Product Purpose

The public manifesto site of a fictional political party inside the server's roleplay. It states the party's twelve goals, makes the case for them, and tells a visitor how to join (by speaking to a party leader in-game). Success: a player can repeat the party's pitch and seeks out a leader in the world.

## Positioning

The only party whose program is a physical build plan as much as a political one: a wall, a railway, a capital that becomes a country. Promises can be shown as things placed in the world, not only as words.

## Operating Context

Roleplay fiction; the party is in-world, the page is out-of-world. Readers know Minecraft. Content is authored by one server member and will be edited by them directly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page, Spanish.
- Party name (confirmed by user): "Frente Perlandés por la Libertad". Motto is still a working title: "Libertad, unidad, orden". Both must live in one place in source.
- Demonym (confirmed): "perlandeses" (perlandés/perlandesa). Use it everywhere; never "perlinianos".
- The nation is Perland; its capital/seat is Perland Village.
- Call to action: speak to a party leader in the game. Leader (confirmed): EstoesMoises. Seat (confirmed): Perland Village, near the Spawn; no exact coordinates. No Discord (user said it is not needed).

## Evidence on Hand

The twelve program points supplied by the user (liberty for all Perland residents; Perland Village as continental power and a country; recover all territories; a wall against monsters; no fear of night, monsters or other players; decentralised power; wealth for all the people; unity; a functional railway; needs covered for everyone; order; rule of law). No logo, leader names, screenshots, membership numbers or election results exist; none may be invented.

## News (confirmed by user mid-build)

The page has a news section. First item: a communiqué answering posters about the user's character (EstoesMoises) that circulate in the city and are, per the user, completely false: a "WANTED" bounty poster accusing "violación de los derechos humanos", and a billboard by a stage with gallows reading "Si no respetas las normas, ya sabes lo que te espera". Authorship of the posters is unknown; the page must not name or accuse anyone. The user supplied two in-game screenshots (not yet on disk).

## Product Principles

- The program is the content; twelve points, none dropped, none reworded into mush.
- Speak as a movement addressing neighbours, not as a corporation or a campaign template.
- Keep the fiction light: it is a game, play it straight and with conviction.
- One clear next step: find a leader in-game.
