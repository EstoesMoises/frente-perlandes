# Surface brief: home

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a perlandés player arriving from Discord or chat; must understand the party, its twelve goals, the false-poster communiqué, and whom to find in-game.

Audience/job: read the program, judge it, seek a leader. Proof: the twelve points, the communiqué. No invented names, numbers or results.

Memorable moment: the plan of Perland draws itself, wall first, railway second.

Unresolved: leader name, in-game coordinates, Discord (placeholders in src/data/frente.ts); screenshots of the false posters (src/assets/carteles/).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The program is a set of civil works drawings. Twelve numbered sheets, one title block, one plan. Refuses the campaign page of hero, tagline and cards.

OWN-WORLD: Plan-sheet paper (warm off-white), delineator ink near-black blue, signal orange for the wall and the approval stamp, rail teal for the railway. Hairline frame with registration ticks, title block cells, revision table, drawn linework at 45 and 90 degrees only, condensed drawing lettering.

STORY: Visitor sees a nation drawn as a build plan, reads that each demand is a sheet with a commitment, sees the party answer false posters with a revision note, and ends at the signature box telling them to find a leader in Perland Village.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Frame with title block across the top (party name large, project line, sheet count 12, scale). Below left two thirds the plan of Perland: octagonal wall around Perland Village, teal railway to stations, dashed outline of territories to recover. Right third legend and the one-sentence pitch. Primary action (find a leader) as a stamped box under the legend.

FORM: Plan de Obras, assigned index 3 of the grounded list, seed key 7f4653fe. Raise from Mapa de Líneas: one ink per theme, angles 45/90, legend as the program's index.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
